Deal Hunter — home-screen concepts

Fresh takes on the new brief (3–4 "deals of the day" like an ad, retailer-first, easy categories, never overwhelming) — built independently by three model "minds": Gemini, Claude, and Codex. Tap to explore on your phone.
Gemini — 1 concept
GEMINI · HOME
Editorial "best 4 today"
"Good afternoon — here are the 4 best deals right now." A calm editorial list of the day's hero deals (price story + honest trust label + a "The Play" sheet), easy category chips up top, then a clean "Choose a store" list with per-store headlines. Faithful to the brief.
Claude — 3 concepts
CLAUDE · BEST
Storefront window
One full-bleed featured deal of the day + a swipe-rail of the other three, then a self-selling "pick a store" list, then a one-tap category filter. Calm, editorial, restrained.
CLAUDE · CREATIVE
Today's Front Page
The app as a daily magazine — the single rarest deal gets a full editorial "cover" with a spotlight sweep; retailers become tappable "channels." Bold but effortless.
CLAUDE · REINTERPRETATION
At home ⇄ In store (intent-led)
One adaptive screen that flips by context: you enter by what you're trying to do (best rare deal / find a product / pick a store / I'm in an aisle), and it reshapes — discovery at home, execution in the aisle.
Codex — 3 concepts
CODEX · BEST
Curated & restrained
Curated deal cards first, simple aisle/category chips, retailer cards with clear headlines, and a calm store-results panel. The faithful, impeccable read of the brief.
CODEX · CREATIVE
Editorial spotlight
A magazine-like hero spotlight with three supporting deal tiles and a bolder store picker — daring, but still simple and store-first.
CODEX · REINTERPRETATION
Goal-first home
Starts from real goals — best rare deal, going to a store, standing in an aisle, watching for something — then adapts the deal and store surface to the goal you pick.
