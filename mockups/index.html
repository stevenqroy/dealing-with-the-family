Deal Hunter — home-screen concepts

Home-screen concepts by three model "minds" — Gemini, Claude, Codex. The newest in each section are marked 📷 real photos — built from a persona evaluation, with real product photography, a store grid, photo-scroll, and the "how to get it" play. The rest use emoji placeholders. Tap any to explore on your phone.
★ Persona-led reinterpretations — newest
CLAUDE · MODE-SWITCH  ·  📷
"What are you here to do?"
Pick Hunt / Stock up / Browse and the same real deals re-lens for that job — rarest + SKU + scan for resellers, by-need + worth-the-trip + one-store for savers, big photos for browsers.
CLAUDE · ONE SURFACE  ·  📷
No picking — calm layered bands
One scroll serves everyone: "Today's wow" (browse), "Worth the trip / shop by need" (saver), "New since you checked + SKUs" (reseller), an honesty note, and a first-timer "here's the whole app" callout.
CODEX · PERSONA-IDENTITY  ·  📷
"Shopping as: Reseller / Saver / Browsing"
Choose who you shop as and the whole home reshapes — the picker itself is the onboarding. Saver gets "worth the trip / one store / by need"; reseller gets rarity + SKU + play; browser gets the photo gallery.
CODEX · PERSONA V2  ·  📷
"Best runs, not endless feeds"
A store-run planner: "Dollar Tree has fresh penny finds" with a real-photo match strip + "Run the play," "Stores worth checking" ranked by run quality, shop-by-need chips, and real-photo top picks. Honest "no live stock."
Gemini — 3 concepts
GEMINI · FRONT DOOR  ·  📷 real photos
Categories-first, single editorial hero
Category chips pinned at the very top, one big ad-style "top pick" hero (real photo), then a clean "Pick a store" list. Tap a store → a real photo grid of that store's deals. Calm and decisive.
GEMINI · CAROUSEL  ·  📷 real photos
Swipeable hero shortlist
A horizontal snap-carousel of the day's best finds (real photos, peek of the next), category chips on top, a "Shop by store" list with store badges, and a "How to get it" bottom sheet. Tap a store → real photo grid.
GEMINI · HOME (v1)
Editorial "best 4 today" (emoji)
The first Gemini take — "Good afternoon, here are the 4 best deals" + category chips + a "Choose a store" list + "The Play" sheet. Emoji imagery (no real photos). Kept for comparison.
Claude — 4 concepts
CLAUDE · V2  ·  📷 real photos
Photo-led, persona-tuned
Real catalog photography: one BIG featured hero, a swipe photo-scroll, smart store list with brand logos, a real photo store-grid, and a deal detail with price story + neutral trust + SKU/scan strip + "How to get it" + privacy-safe Share. Built to serve all four personas. Newest.
CLAUDE · BEST
Storefront window
One full-bleed featured deal of the day + a swipe-rail of the other three, then a self-selling "pick a store" list, then a one-tap category filter. Calm, editorial, restrained.
CLAUDE · CREATIVE
Today's Front Page
The app as a daily magazine — the single rarest deal gets a full editorial "cover" with a spotlight sweep; retailers become tappable "channels." Bold but effortless.
CLAUDE · REINTERPRETATION
At home ⇄ In store (intent-led)
One adaptive screen that flips by context: you enter by what you're trying to do (best rare deal / find a product / pick a store / I'm in an aisle), and it reshapes — discovery at home, execution in the aisle.
Codex — 4 concepts
CODEX · V2  ·  📷 real photos
Photo-led with search
Real catalog photos: a big featured hero ("updated 4 min ago"), a "Photo-scroll · swipe the real finds" carousel, "Shop by need" chips, a search bar, and a "Choose a store" list → real photo grid. Sentence-case, neutral trust, honest signals. Newest.
CODEX · BEST
Curated & restrained
Curated deal cards first, simple aisle/category chips, retailer cards with clear headlines, and a calm store-results panel. The faithful, impeccable read of the brief.
CODEX · CREATIVE
Editorial spotlight
A magazine-like hero spotlight with three supporting deal tiles and a bolder store picker — daring, but still simple and store-first.
CODEX · REINTERPRETATION
Goal-first home
Starts from real goals — best rare deal, going to a store, standing in an aisle, watching for something — then adapts the deal and store surface to the goal you pick.
Standalone direction — real photos
HOME · BEST-EFFORT  ·  📷
Storefront first, dashboard later
