Independent direction 1

Storefront first, dashboard later

This version makes the opening screen do two things only: sell the best few deals of the day, then make the next retailer tap obvious. It keeps the warm Deal Hunter palette, but lowers the visual noise and gives one product a true ad-agency moment.

Why each element earns its place

  • Top search: supports "I need a product" without competing with the curated home story.
  • Hero deal: creates the "holy smokes" moment with image, penny price, was-price, and one honest confidence row.
  • Three mini deals: proves there are multiple real opportunities today without turning the page into a feed.
  • Retailer cards: answer "which store should I care about?" with a headline, not a count dump.
  • Category chips: visible, finger-sized, and immediate. Tap Cleaning or Kids to reshape the page; tap Pets for the calm empty state.
  • The play: each deal has a short route-to-success hook, leaving room for the future authored deal instructions and sharing flow.

Interaction proof

  • Tap a retailer card to enter that store's best-deals view.
  • Tap any category chip to filter the home and store view.
  • Tap a deal for the single deal card sheet with price story, trust, play, and share/watch affordances.
  • The Pets chip shows the empty state; Check again briefly shows loading.

Honesty treatment

Every deal uses one compact trust row: freshness, match/report confidence, and "check tag" or "scan to confirm." It never claims stock and never invents verification.
